The Life Manual

What goes into it, how we build it, and what happens when we're done.

A Life Manual is built with you, chapter by chapter, until the person who has to step in can find what matters and understand what to do next.

Start with the Blueprint. Then the real work begins.

The Blueprint Session maps where you actually stand and gives you a 72-hour action plan. If you continue into a Life Manual, the next step is getting the working system in place before we start building the chapters.

The goal is a usable manual, not a pile of paperwork.

The Life Manual connects the information, systems, records, people, instructions, and decisions someone would need if you were suddenly unavailable. It gives a successor a place to start and a path to follow.

What happens after the Blueprint Session

01

Agreement, authorization, and payment

You receive and sign the Client Services Agreement and the Digital Setup and Continuity Authorization. Payment is completed, and the engagement moves from planning into the build.

02

We set up the secure working system

Craig walks you through setting up the secure communications system you will use for the work. That includes encrypted email, a password manager, and secure cloud storage. Proton is one example of the type of system used, but the process is organized around the function, not a particular vendor.

03

Your Secure Drive gets the working materials

Once the cloud drive is ready, the Client Guidebook, Successor Roadmap, Preparation Checklist, and folder shell are transferred into it so you have a structure for gathering the information and records that belong in the Manual.

04

The Client Portal is activated

You are enrolled in the Client Portal, Craig walks you through it, and the next working session is scheduled. The portal gives you a place to see the Manual develop chapter by chapter.

05

We verify the access structure

You log back into the password manager and confirm that the credentials for your email, cloud drive, and Client Portal have been added. The Manual can point to where access lives without becoming a document full of passwords.

06

We start with Digital Life and Emergency & Successor Orientation

The first two chapters establish the digital map and the immediate orientation a successor needs. From there, the Manual continues one chapter at a time.

The first two chapters set the foundation

Chapter 1: Digital Life

Digital Life is a high-level map of the digital systems your life depends on. It covers the operational role of each system, the authority structure around it, where recovery information is located, and what happens when someone else has to take over.

  • Cloud Storage
  • Communication & Messaging
  • Devices & Operating Systems
  • Digital Financial Accounts
  • Password Manager
  • Primary E-Mail
  • Subscriptions & Renewals
  • 2FA & Recovery Codes
  • Online Presence

Chapter 2: Emergency & Successor Orientation

This chapter is built for moments of urgency or uncertainty. Its job is to reduce confusion and establish calm direction when you cannot be the person making the next call.

  • Emergency Contacts
  • First 48-Hours Plan
  • Child Care & Dependents

The First 48-Hours Plan establishes the immediate sequence: secure the home, access the Life Manual, contact key people, secure financial systems, and review the legal documents that apply.

Then the rest of the Manual gets built

Every session after the foundation builds another chapter. The work stays structured, the draft is reviewed as it develops, and the password manager and Secure Drive are checked rather than assumed. Personal covers six chapters. Business adds Business Continuity and covers all seven.

What a successor actually gets

The finished Manual gives a successor an organized map of what exists, where to find it, who matters, what happens first, and where to look next. It does not become a password dump. The Manual identifies the password manager and the high-level access path while keeping private credentials out of the document.

The Successor Roadmap provides additional direction for the first 48 hours, the first week, and coordination with the appropriate professional advisers. It does not grant legal authority. Legal authority still comes from properly executed legal instruments.

When the Manual is finished

The final session brings your successor into the room for a walkthrough while you are still available to answer questions. After that walkthrough, you have 72 hours to review the finished work and fix anything that still needs attention.

After the review period, the working material is removed from the portal under the Zero-Knowledge Protocol. The Manual remains yours. When life changes enough to matter, an Annual Review can bring it back up to date.

The Life Manual sits beside your legal and professional planning.

It organizes practical information and provides operational direction. It does not replace a will, trust, power of attorney, attorney, accountant, financial professional, or other adviser. Where legal authority or professional advice is required, the Manual points the successor toward the appropriate records and people.
