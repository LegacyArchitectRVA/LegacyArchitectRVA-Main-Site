Questions, answered

The things people ask before they start.

The Service
What is a Life Manual?
It's one document holding everything somebody would need if you weren't reachable. Accounts, contacts, records, instructions, all in one place instead of scattered across your head and a drawer. Whoever steps in can act instead of guess.
Is this a will?
No. A will decides who gets what. A Life Manual explains how the accounts, the house, and the daily stuff actually work so somebody can pick it up. You probably want both. One's legal, one's practical.
Do I need a lawyer?
Not for this. A Life Manual isn't legal advice and it isn't a legal document. You should still see an attorney about your will or power of attorney, and we'd tell you to. This sits alongside that work, it doesn't stand in for it.
Who is this for?
People whose absence would leave everybody else scrambling. The one in the family who handles the accounts and the renewals. Parents who'd rather their kids grieve than fight paperwork. Business owners who know the place doesn't run without them.
Privacy & Security
What is the Zero-Knowledge Standard?
It means we don't see, store, or hold your private information. Your documents live in your own Secure Drive. We help build the structure. You decide what goes in it, where it sits, and who gets access.
Does the manual contain my passwords?
No, and that's deliberate. The manual points to where your credentials live, like your password manager, and who has the authority to get in. It tells somebody how to find access. It never spells out the access itself. A document with every password in it would be the most dangerous thing in your house.
Do you store my personal information?
No. The system uses a client-controlled Secure Drive. Your information stays entirely under your control, and anything you submit during facilitation is purged within 72 hours of delivery.
How does information get to you, and who's responsible for it?
Nothing sensitive travels by plain email. Anything shared during a session moves through encrypted channels and is purged from the system within 72 hours. I never take custody of your accounts, passwords, or funds, and I'm not the one holding them, you are. Legacy Architect RVA is a licensed local business, not a bank, a law firm, or an advisory practice. I organize what exists and where access lives. I don't hold it, and I don't touch it.
Getting Started
How long does it take?
Under 30 days is typical. You go at your own pace, pillar by pillar. Nothing says you have to do it all at once.
What if I already have a will and a trust?
Good, you're ahead of most people. Those cover ownership and authority. A Life Manual covers the layer they don't reach: the accounts, the passwords, the bills, the routines, the vendors. It's the piece that makes everything else usable when it counts.

The free Readiness Check. Two minutes, and it shows you exactly where your gaps are. From there most people take the Blueprint Session, where we go through those results together and you leave with a 72-hour action plan. The $249 credits in full toward a Life Manual, so it isn't money spent twice.
