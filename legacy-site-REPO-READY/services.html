What happens after you sign

This isn't a form you fill out once. It's real sessions, one after another, until the whole thing actually holds together. Here's exactly how it goes, no skipped steps.

1

You sign, and it's real

Not someday, not once you get around to it. Your name goes on the page and the actual work starts before the ink's dry.

2

Your Secure Drive goes up before you ever sit down

It's built first, checklist and successor roadmap already loaded in, so you walk into session one with something to work from instead of an empty screen.

3

Session one, and the vault becomes yours

You learn where everything lives, your portal turns on, and you start Digital Life together. From there you watch your own progress fill in.

4

Every session after that builds a chapter

One chapter at a time, no skipping ahead. Whether you're really using the password manager and the Drive gets checked, not assumed because you said so once and then forgot by Tuesday.

5

The last session brings your successor into the room

Not a phone call after the fact. Not a folder they find later and have to decode alone. You walk it together, once, while you're both still here to ask the questions that matter.

6

72 hours, then it's locked

You get three days after the walkthrough to fix anything that still bugs you. After that, everything comes off the portal for good under the Zero-Knowledge Protocol. It was always yours. Nobody keeps a copy behind you.
